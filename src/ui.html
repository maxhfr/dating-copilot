Automatic sessions

Runs start by themselves at these times while this app is open. Scheduled runs keep the random skip and quiet hours. Limits protect the account: at most 4 a day, 3+ hours apart.

The Mac must be awake with the app open. While scheduling is on, the app keeps your Mac from sleeping on its own (the screen can still turn off; closing the lid still sleeps it). A time missed while asleep runs once, late, when the Mac wakes, never within 2 hours of another session.

    Stop the session before saving. Filters write to config/rubric.yaml; the threshold to config/scoring.yaml. Scoring weights are not editable here.
