Automatic sessions

Runs start at these times while this app is open. Scheduled runs keep the random skip and quiet hours. Limits protect the account: at most 4 a day, 3+ hours apart.

The Mac must be awake with the app open at these times. The app keeps your Mac awake only while a session is running; otherwise it sleeps normally. A time missed while asleep runs once, late, when the Mac wakes, never within 2 hours of another session. Closing the lid still sleeps the Mac.

    Settings

    Filters, threshold and limits. Stop the session before saving.

    Filters write to config/rubric.yaml; the threshold to config/scoring.yaml. Scoring weights are not editable here.
