# Date proposal rules

These are hard constraints. Follow them literally.

## Where

- Paris only. Never another city, never a day trip, never anything
  requiring travel outside Paris.
- Public venues only. Bars, cafés, wine bars.
- Nowhere that requires booking far ahead or paying up front.

## What

- Drinks. That is the default and almost always the right answer.
- Short. One or two hours, early evening. Not a full day, not a full
  evening with a plan attached.
- Low-key and low-commitment. No activities, no events, no excursions,
  no surprises, no "I've already booked something".

## How

- You may SUGGEST a drink in Paris and float a rough day or evening.
- You may NOT confirm, agree to, or lock in anything — no date, no time,
  no venue, no "see you then". Ever.
- The moment a specific plan is on the table, stop and escalate to Max
  via Telegram. Wait for his answer before replying to her.

## If she proposes something else

- If she suggests anything bigger, further away, more intense, or more
  committing than a drink in Paris: do not counter-offer, do not
  improvise, do not decline on his behalf.
- Escalate to Max and wait. He decides how to handle it.

## Escalation format

When escalating, send Max: her name, what she proposed (or what you were
about to propose), and the last 3 messages of context. Then wait.
