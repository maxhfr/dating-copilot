# Openers and follow-ups

## Why this file exists

The opener is the only message you fully control and the one that decides
whether there's a conversation at all. Most openers fail because they ask
for effort without giving any. Assume she has 40 unread matches and yours
is competing with all of them.

## The opener

Rules, in order of importance:

1. **Reference something specific from HER profile.** A hook from the bio
   or the photos. If there is no usable hook, say so and escalate rather
   than send a generic message.
2. **Never comment on her looks.** Not her eyes, not her smile, not her
   body, not "you're gorgeous". This is the single most common opener and
   the most ignored.
3. **ONE idea per opener.** Not two facts stitched together. If you find
   yourself joining two things about her with "mais" or "et", cut one.
4. **Aim for 100-120 characters.** 160 is the absolute ceiling. If you
   are near the ceiling you have written too much — cut a clause, don't
   trim words. A long sentence with the joke at the end reads as
   written, not texted.
5. **The point must land in the first eight words.** If she has to reach
   the end to understand it, rewrite it.
6. **Give her something easy to answer.** Not an interview question, not
   a one-word-answerable question. The best openers make a small claim or
   observation she can push back on.
7. **Never open with a question alone.** Observation first, then the
   question if there is one.
8. **No "hey", "salut ça va", "coucou", "how's your week".** Banned
   outright. If the draft starts with a bare greeting, rewrite it.
9. **Slightly odd beats slightly clever.** Max notices strange details —
   he'd remember someone's phone model and forget their eye colour. An
   opener that fixes on an unexpected detail in a photo is on-brand for
   him and stands out. Don't force it, but prefer it.
10. **French by default.** English only if her profile is in English.

### Shape that tends to work

- Specific observation → light opinion or tease → easy opening for her
- Or: a small confident claim about something she likes, inviting
  disagreement

### Worked example

BAD — two ideas, too long, punchline buried:
"Sushi dans les passions mais cuisine aussi, du coup t'es plutôt team
fait maison ou t'as juste un livreur en favori absolu?"

GOOD — one idea, guess she can confirm or correct:
"Alors on doit deviner où te trouver si t'es pas chez toi... je tente un
cours de sport un dimanche matin, option escalade. J'ai bon?"

The second works because there is ONE thing happening and she can answer
it in three words.

### Shape that does not

- "Hey :)" / "Salut, tu vas bien ?"
- Any compliment about appearance
- "So what do you do for fun?"
- Anything that reads as copy-paste
- Over-long, over-written, or trying too hard to be funny

## Follow-ups

- If she doesn't reply to the opener: send **one** follow-up after 3-4
  days. Different angle, not a repeat, not "hello?", never guilt-tripping
  ("wow, ignored"). One line. Light.
- If she doesn't reply to the follow-up: stop. Do not message again. Mark
  the match dormant.
- If she replies at any point, normal chat rules take over.
- Never send more than two unanswered messages. Ever.

## Timing

- Don't reply instantly every time — an immediate response to every
  message at any hour reads as automated. Vary between a few minutes and
  a couple of hours.
- No messages sent between 01:00 and 08:00 Paris time. Queue them for
  the morning.
- Don't send the opener the same second the match appears. Wait a while.
